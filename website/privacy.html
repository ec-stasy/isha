Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Operated by [YOUR LEGAL NAME / COMPANY], [COUNTRY]

Isha is built on a simple promise: your data stays on your machine. Isha is free and fully local, so there is remarkably little to say here — which is the point.

1. The app collects nothing

The Isha desktop application runs entirely on your computer. It contains no telemetry, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. It makes no network connection unless you explicitly ask it to — for example, by choosing “Check for updates” or by sending an issue report you have reviewed. Your commands, clipboard, files, and usage are never transmitted anywhere.

2. No account, no purchase, no personal data

Isha is free to download and use. There is no account to create, no license key, and no checkout — so we collect no name, email, or payment information from you. If you download from a third-party host (such as GitHub Releases), that host’s own privacy policy governs the download itself.

3. Issue reports (only if you send one)

If you choose to send an issue report from within the app, it bundles diagnostic information — your operating-system version and a short, redacted log of recent commands — into a file that is shown to you in full before anything is sent. Nothing is ever sent automatically, and sensitive content is redacted before it is written to the log.

4. Updates

When you choose to check for or install an update, Isha contacts our update host to fetch a signed manifest and, if you confirm, the installer. This exchange transmits no personal data — only the request needed to retrieve the update — and the download is signature-verified before it runs.

5. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side. If you ever have a privacy question, email us at support@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.
