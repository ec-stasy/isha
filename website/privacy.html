Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Operated by [YOUR LEGAL NAME / COMPANY], [COUNTRY]

Isha is built on a simple promise: your data stays on your machine. This policy explains the very small amount of information we do handle, and why.

1. The app collects nothing

The Isha desktop application runs entirely on your computer. It contains no telemetry, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. It makes no network connection unless you explicitly ask it to — for example, by choosing “Check for updates” or by sending an issue report you have reviewed. Your commands, clipboard, files, and usage are never transmitted anywhere.

2. What we hold, and why

When you buy Isha, our payment partner (our Merchant of Record — see below) collects the information needed to complete the sale: your name, email address, and billing details. We receive from them only your name and email address, which we retain for one purpose: to deliver your license key and, if you want them, product updates and important notices.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card details — those are handled entirely by our payment partner.

3. Payment processing

Payments are processed by [Lemon Squeezy / Paddle / your MoR], who act as the Merchant of Record for your purchase. They handle billing, tax, and card data under their own privacy policy, which applies to the checkout. Please review it at [MoR privacy policy URL].

4. Issue reports (only if you send one)

If you choose to send an issue report from within the app, it bundles diagnostic information — your operating-system version and a short, redacted log of recent commands — into a file that is shown to you in full before anything is sent. Nothing is ever sent automatically, and sensitive content is redacted before it is written to the log.

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Since all we hold is your name and email, honoring this is straightforward. Email us at support@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.

6. Data retention

We keep your name and email for as long as your license is valid and we are delivering updates, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, email registered customers.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@YOUR-DOMAIN.com.
