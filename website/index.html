Questions

Does Isha send my data anywhere?

No. It runs entirely on your PC and makes no network request unless you explicitly ask it to — for example, to check for an update. There’s no analytics or telemetry of any kind. Read our privacy policy and threat model for the details.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). Isha is a lightweight background app — it sits in your system tray and uses only tens of megabytes of RAM.

Is it a subscription?

No. It’s a one-time purchase. You get updates for the version you bought, and you own your copy.

How do I get my copy after buying?

Right after checkout you’ll get an email with your download link and your license key. Open Isha, press the palette hotkey, and type activate license <your key>. That’s it — activation happens offline.

Windows warned me about the installer — is it safe?

Newly released installers can trip SmartScreen until they build reputation; a code-signed release clears this. If you ever see a warning, verify you downloaded from this site and continue. See our terms.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — 14 days, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

What's the difference between Isha Lite and full Isha?

Same installer, same app. Isha Lite (free, no license) opens/closes apps and controls volume & brightness. Activating a license unlocks everything else — Modes, window management, reminders, clipboard history, Pomodoro, voice, and more. See the full list on How it works.
